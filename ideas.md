# Nexus A Liverton Store — Design Ground Truth

## Ground Truth Brand Identity

Brand Name: **Nexus A Liverton Store** (or Nexus Store)
Core Offerings: **Smart Home** and **Beauty & Wellness**

## Chosen Direction: Modern Technological & Elevated Lifestyle Editorial

### Core Principles

1. **Precision & Innovation:** Clean, warm parchment base combined with brass and slate accents to showcase modern smart home innovations and elevated beauty & wellness essentials.
2. **Curated Simplicity:** Highlighting products through focused, high-contrast typography and intentional spacing.
3. **Designed for Everyday Living:** Every interaction is intuitive, responsive, and tailored for modern shoppers.

### Typography System

- **DM Serif Display** for editorial headlines and brand identity moments.
- **Manrope** for primary navigation, product titles, specs, body text, and UI controls.

### Brand Essence

Nexus A Liverton Store is a premier lifestyle and tech house specializing in Smart Home automation and Beauty & Wellness innovations. Personality: **innovative, elevated, considered**.
